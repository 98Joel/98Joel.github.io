01 Overview

Key-Shifter keeps the musical decision simple: choose a direction, keep playing.

Changing keys should not interrupt a performance. This project explores a focused control surface for remapping notes while preserving a player's familiar physical relationship with the keyboard.

02 Design principles

Complex behavior,
calm interface.

The strongest idea behind Key-Shifter is restraint: surface only the choices that matter in the moment, then make the system's response easy to understand.

  1. 01

    Make state visible

    A clear before-and-after mapping removes guesswork. The user should always know where a note is going.

    ↗
  2. 02

    Favor physical intuition

    Controls follow the musician's mental model: lower, raise, play. The interaction asks for no translation layer.

    ↗
  3. 03

    Close the feedback loop

    Labels, motion, and sound reinforce the same outcome, making experimentation feel low-risk and fast.
