02 Workflow to document

Show the handoffs,
not just the UI.

The case study focuses on the full technical chain: source material, isolated musical part, Play Along interaction, and a recorded explanation of the choices between each step.

  1. 01

    Begin with the musical part

    A separate source-separation program extracts the piano or bass from a track. The tutorial will start here so the audience can see why Play Along has a focused input to work with.

    ↗
  2. 02

    Make the handoff explicit

    The screen recording will show what moves from extraction into Play Along, where the user makes a musical decision, and how the controls respond.

    ↗
  3. 03

    Use the recording as evidence

    Rather than claiming a capability in marketing copy, the walkthrough will let the reviewer watch the workflow, inspect the interface, and follow the result end to end.

    ↗
03 Walkthrough recording

The tutorial becomes the proof of process.

When the screen recording is ready, it will sit here as the central artifact of the case study. The surrounding notes turn it into a quick, reviewable technical walkthrough rather than a product demo.
