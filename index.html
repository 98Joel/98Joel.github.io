About

Short version.

I am a computer engineering student at the University of Oklahoma, graduating in 2028. Most of what I build starts as something I wanted to exist — a practice tool, a task board my team craved — and then turns into an exercise in making it hold up for someone else.

I like problems that sit between hardware, signal processing, and interface design, where the correct answer is not only "does it run" but "does it stay understandable at 2am when something breaks."
